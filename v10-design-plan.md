# Snakes v10 — Design Plan

## Overview

Seven features selected from 26 ideas generated by 3 ideation agents (gameplay, meta/retention, polish/accessibility). Selection criteria: impact × feasibility / risk, cross-feature synergy, and coverage across all three angles. Total estimated ~890 LOC.

v9 baseline: commit `21622d5`, ~7648 lines, 19-test suite. The game has: render scheduler, evolution, quests, visual polish, snapshots, PWA, HiDPI, calm mode, settings panel, recap card, hunter food, bounty rush, save export/import, auto-save, combo aura, portal food, golden hoard, visual feedback, diary replay.

## Feature A — Combo Bank

**Concept:** At each combo milestone (5, 10, 20, 30), the player banks 1 "Combo Charge" (cap 5) instead of the milestone being purely cosmetic. Charges are spent via the `Z` key on one-shot tactical boosts:
- **Clear** (1 charge): destroy all obstacles within a 3-tile radius of the head
- **Magnet Pulse** (1 charge): instantly pull all foods 6 tiles toward the head
- **Food Teleport** (1 charge): relocate the current food to a free tile near the head

**Why:** Converts the passive combo multiplier into an active resource decision. "Save for a desperation clear, or spend now to keep the combo chain alive?" This is the previously-rejected "Combo Chain Store" idea, now ripe because `handleComboMilestone()` (line ~5685) and `pushTransientRing`/`queueToast` infrastructure make it cheap.

**LOC:** ~140

**Scores:** Impact 4, Feasibility 5, Risk 2, Synergy 5

**Hook points:**
- `comboCharges` (int) — new module-scope state, declared beside `comboCount` (~line 688). Reset in `resetGame()` + `resetWaveState()`.
- `handleComboMilestone()` (line ~5685) — increment `comboCharges` when `COMBO_MILESTONES.includes(comboCount)`, after the existing floater. Cap at 5.
- `spendComboCharge(kind)` — new function, triggered by `Z` key in keydown handler (gated on `gameState === 'playing'` and `comboCharges > 0`).
  - `'clear'`: filter `obstacles[]` removing those within 3 tiles, `spawnParticles` + `pushTransientRing`
  - `'magnet_pulse'`: reuse the magnet-pull loop (line ~6340) with radius `6*TILE_SIZE` and instant snap
  - `'food_teleport'`: call `findFreeTile(snake[0].x, snake[0].y)` and reassign `food.x/y`
- HUD: draw charge count in `drawGame()` near the combo display (small canvas badge after `drawXPBar`).
- Snapshot: additive `comboCharges` field (no `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` bump). No session-end hook (dies with run, like bounty).

## Feature B — Bomb Food

**Concept:** A new `FOOD_TIERS.bomb` food (weight 0.04). Eating it detonates a 2-tile-radius blast that clears obstacles and trims the rival snake's tail by 3 segments. The bomb gives 0 score — the reward is board-state manipulation. If nothing is in range, it's wasted (risk/reward).

**Why:** A tactical tool, not a points source. "Wait until obstacles box you in, or until the rival is adjacent for the tail-trim." Synergizes with progressive obstacles and the rival system.

**LOC:** ~120

**Scores:** Impact 4, Feasibility 5, Risk 2, Synergy 4

**Hook points:**
- `FOOD_TIERS.bomb` — new entry: `{ pts: 0, xp: 30, weight: 0.04, color: '#FF6600', pCount: 16, sound: 'bonus', glow: 12 }`. Rebalance: reduce `common` 0.37→0.34 and `bonus` 0.13→0.11 to keep cumulative sum ≤ 1.0 (`pickRandomFoodType` has no normalization — per v8 E).
- Eat block (line ~6004) — `food.type === 'bomb'` branch BEFORE score award: iterate `obstacles[]` removing within `2*TILE_SIZE` Manhattan, `spawnParticles` + `pushTransientRing(wrappedHead, 80, '#FF6600', 500, 'ring', 4)`. For each rival in range, `pop()` 3 tail segments.
- `drawFood()` (line ~4285) — `bomb` case: pulsing dark-orange circle with fuse spark. Draw at local origin per D1 discipline.
- Echo-brew `food2` coerces `food.type === 'bomb'` to `'common'` (same as hunter/portal).
- `EXPLORER` threshold stays at `sessionFoodTypes.size >= 6` (now 6-of-8 non-Walled) — do NOT bump.
- Bomb excluded in Walled mode (obstacles disabled, so blast is pointless) via the `effectiveTiers` filter (same path as hunter exclusion).
- Snapshot: no new state (bomb is a food tier, already in `food.type`).

## Feature C — Hazard Zones

**Concept:** Periodic expanding danger tiles ("lava"/"shadow rifts") that pulse on and off in random board regions. Lingering on an active hazard tile for ≥2 ticks kills the snake (respecting shield/vampire/serpentine). Hazards expand in radius over their lifetime, then contract and vanish — a breathing spatial-threat distinct from static obstacles.

**Why:** Forces dynamic rerouting. Unlike obstacles (permanent, placed at spawn), hazards are temporal — you can wait them out or race through during their off-phase. "Do I commit to this lane knowing a hazard is pulsing?" Combined with the rival pushing you toward hazards, and hoard foods spawning inside active zones, the risk/reward tension is emergent.

**LOC:** ~180

**Scores:** Impact 4, Feasibility 4, Risk 3, Synergy 5

**Hook points:**
- `hazards[]` array (entries: `{cx, cy, radius, phase, lifeTimer, maxLife}`) — new module-scope state, declared early (~line 204). Reset in `resetGame()` + `resetWaveState()`.
- `MODE_CONFIG[mode].hazardsEnabled` — new flag, added to Classic, Time Attack, Daily, Snap (mirrors `hoardEnabled`). Excluded in Zen, Walled, Gauntlet.
- `tickHazards()` — new function called from `updateGame()` after `tickBounty()` (line ~5672). Advances phase timers, manages expand/contract lifecycle, spawns new hazards on a tick counter (like `HOARD_INTERVAL`).
- `checkPlayerDeath()` (line ~5883) — hazard check: if `wrappedHead` within `radius` AND `phase === 'on'` AND `hazardLingerCounter >= 2`, return true (death). Shield/vampire/serpentine intercept in existing order. Add `isHazardCollision(wrappedHead)` alongside `isObstacleCollision` for Serpentine gating (hazard death is obstacle-like for the pass).
- `drawHazards()` — new helper, drawn between `drawObstacles()` and `drawFood()` in `drawGame()`. Radial gradient + pulsing alpha, gated by `calmModeEffective()` for the pulse.
- `generateFood()` (line ~1792) — add `hazards` to collision-rejection `currentSnake` parameter (same pattern as `extraFoods`).
- Snapshot: additive `hazards` field.

## Feature D — Daily Seed Leaderboard

**Concept:** A personal history leaderboard for the Daily Challenge — every daily run is ranked against your own past attempts on the same deterministic seed. The same calendar day always shows the same seed; you chase your day-best. Combined with the existing streak system, it gives two reasons to return daily.

**Why:** The daily seed is already shared across all players (same date → same seed → same layout). A personal leaderboard turns "one daily run" into "beat your day-best, then come back tomorrow when the seed rotates." The rotating seed is the hook.

**LOC:** ~140

**Scores:** Impact 4, Feasibility 5, Risk 1, Synergy 5

**Hook points:**
- `snake_daily_lb` — localStorage JSON map keyed by date string from `getDayString()` (line ~1282), storing an array of `{score, time, foodEaten, maxCombo, ts}` (top 5 per day).
- Write in `finalizeSession()` (line ~2953) gated on `gameMode === 'daily'` — add to BOTH `gameOver()` and `endZenSession()` per the Zen-Mode-Gap rule.
- Render the day's top-5 in `showStatsPanel()` (line ~6854) or a sub-section of the daily HUD.
- Reuses the diary's `saveDiaryEntry()` pattern but is seed-keyed rather than chronological.
- No snapshot/tick-loop changes — pure session-end persistence.

## Feature E — Meta-Achievements

**Concept:** A second-tier achievement layer: "meta-achievements" that require completing sets of existing achievements (e.g. "Combo Trinity" = COMBO_10 + COMBO_20 + COMBO_50; "Apex Predator" = all evolution achievements; "Completionist" = all achievements). Each grants a large XP + token bundle and an exclusive cosmetic.

**Why:** The current `ACHIEVEMENTS` object is a flat list; once mostly unlocked, the system goes dormant. Meta-achievements re-activate it by giving completionists a higher-order goal and rewarding cross-cutting playstyles. They also steer new players toward varied modes/behaviors.

**LOC:** ~120

**Scores:** Impact 3, Feasibility 5, Risk 1, Synergy 4

**Hook points:**
- `META_ACHIEVEMENTS` map (same shape as `ACHIEVEMENTS`: `{name, desc, xp, icon, check(s,c)}`) whose `check` reads the `achievements` object, e.g. `check = (s,c) => achievements.COMBO_10 && achievements.COMBO_20 && achievements.COMBO_50`.
- Evaluate in `checkAchievements()` (line ~6652) as a second loop after the existing one, using the same `unlockedAny`/`queueToast`/`mutatorTokens++`/`saveManager.set` reward path (line ~6676).
- Persist in a parallel `snake_meta_achievements` JSON key (meta IDs namespaced `meta_*` to avoid collisions).
- Cosmetics reuse the `unlockedCosmetics` path.
- No tick-loop, snapshot, or session-end changes — the food-eat-tick `checkAchievements(score)` call site (line ~2276) already runs it.

## Feature F — Colorblind Food Palettes

**Concept:** 3 selectable colorblind palettes (Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia) that remap food-tier colors + snake/obstacle hues to perceptually-distinct, confusion-line-safe sets (Okabe-Ito palette). Persisted via saveManager, selectable in Settings.

**Why:** Food tiers currently encode rarity via red/green/gold — red-green confusion (most common CVD) makes "common vs bonus" hard to distinguish. High-contrast mode exists but is a single blunt toggle. Each food tier already has a distinct SHAPE (circle/diamond/chevron/paw) which helps, but color is the primary cue.

**LOC:** ~140

**Scores:** Impact 4, Feasibility 4, Risk 2, Synergy 5

**Hook points:**
- `CVD_PALETTES` object keyed by type (protan/deutan/tritan/off), each mapping food-type → {fill, outline} from Okabe-Ito (blue #0072B2, orange #E69F00, magenta #CC79A7, yellow #F0E442, etc.).
- Persist via `saveManager.set('snake_cvd_palette', 'off')`, loaded near `highContrast` (line ~263).
- `foodColor(type)` helper — checks palette first, falls back to `FOOD_TIERS[type].color`. Used in `drawFood()` (line ~4295), `drawExtraFood()` (line ~4523), `drawSnake()` skin path (line ~4628).
- 4-button selector in `renderSettings()` (line ~1549) mirroring the Calm Mode Auto/On/Off buttons.
- Invalidate `gridCacheTheme` on change (like `toggleHighContrast` at line ~1604) so the grid rebuilds.

## Feature G — Input Buffer Queue (2-deep)

**Concept:** Replace the single-slot `nextDirection` buffer with a 2-deep queue so a fast double-tap (e.g. up-then-left around a corner) registers both turns across consecutive ticks instead of dropping the second.

**Why:** `nextDirection` (line ~260) is a single slot — the last press wins. On fast corner sequences the second input arrives before the next tick consumes the first, overwriting it and dropping the turn. This is the #1 input-feel complaint in twitch snake games. The D-pad and swipe paths both write `nextDirection` directly, so they share the bug.

**LOC:** ~50

**Scores:** Impact 5, Feasibility 3, Risk 3, Synergy 4

**Hook points:**
- Replace `let nextDirection = null` with `let dirQueue = []` (cap 2).
- `setNextDirection()` (line ~3622) pushes `{dx, dy}` if not the reverse of the queue tail's effective direction; the tick loop (line ~5847) shifts from the front.
- Reverse-check: compare against the LAST consumed direction if the queue is empty, else the queue tail — prevents the "queue two reverses and self-collide" bug.
- Touch swipe handler (line ~7455+) calls the same `setNextDirection`.
- Update BOTH the keydown movement switch (line ~7380) and every `nextDirection = null` reset site (lines ~2087, 5137) to `dirQueue = []`.
- Snapshot serialization (line ~2417) stores `dirQueue` instead of `nextDirection` (additive, no `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` bump).
- Replay recording (`recordReplayDirection` at line ~5852) logs per-tick consumed direction — unchanged.

## Implementation Order

Features have minimal cross-dependencies. Suggested order (least risk first, building up):

1. **G. Input Buffer Queue** (~50 LOC) — foundational input fix, touches the tick loop but is self-contained. Test first with rapid corner sequences.
2. **F. Colorblind Food Palettes** (~140 LOC) — pure rendering, no game-logic change. Safe.
3. **E. Meta-Achievements** (~120 LOC) — additive checks on existing state. No tick-loop/snapshot changes.
4. **D. Daily Seed Leaderboard** (~140 LOC) — session-end persistence only. No tick-loop changes.
5. **B. Bomb Food** (~120 LOC) — new food tier, touches the eat block + food weights. After F so palette helper exists.
6. **A. Combo Bank** (~140 LOC) — new active resource, touches combo milestones + keydown. After B so the obstacle-clear charge can synergize with bomb's obstacle-clear.
7. **C. Hazard Zones** (~180 LOC) — most complex: new tick-loop system, draw order, collision checks. Last so all prior systems are stable.

## Out of Scope

- **Snake Split** (220 LOC, R4) — signature "wow" mechanic but collision logic for two independent snakes is non-trivial. Revisit for v10.5 after playtesting the combo/bomb/hazard trio.
- **Evolution Branching** (200 LOC, R4) — build diversity is huge but the pause-for-choice flow interrupts game tempo. Better as a v11 candidate with careful UX design.
- **Hoard Overload** (150 LOC, R3) — high-synergy climax modifier on the existing hoard. Strong candidate for v10.5 — it extends an already-shipped system rather than adding a new dimension.
- **Phantom Rival** (160 LOC, R4) — most personal antagonist but fairness tuning (ghost catching player unfairly) is risky. Needs playtesting infrastructure first.
- **Seasonal Ladder** (220 LOC) — highest-impact single meta feature but needs the daily loop proven first. Natural v11 flagship.
- **Prestige Perk Tree** (320 LOC, R3) — highest-impact long-horizon feature but touches many systems. Better as an early v11 candidate.
- **Contract Wagers** (260 LOC, R3) — economy balance risk. Needs the token economy to have a sink first (currently stalls after unlocks).
- **Run Code Cards** (180 LOC) — social-comparison substrate, but needs a social feature to be meaningful. Bundle with whichever social feature ships first (v11).
- **Weekly Seeded Gauntlet** (240 LOC, R3) — needs gauntlet wave-gen audit for `Math.random()` bypasses. Moderate risk.
- **Per-Mode Mastery** (200 LOC) — solid retention but lower synergy than the selected features. v11 candidate.
- **Keyboard Remapping** (120 LOC) — valuable accessibility, but lower synergy and the `s` key collision fix is a side-effect, not a driver. v10.5 candidate.
- **Adaptive Quality Scaling** (90 LOC, R3) — risks degrading visuals unpredictably; needs careful threshold tuning. v10.5 candidate.
- **Web Share API** (60-90 LOC) — very cheap and high-Net, but the recap card is already information-dense. Consider bundling with Run Code Cards in v11 when there's a social payload to share.
- **Recap Card Stagger-In** (50-70 LOC) — pure polish, very low risk. Could be bundled as a bonus if time permits.
- **Save Corruption Auto-Recovery** (70 LOC) — valuable data-safety but orthogonal to the v10 gameplay/meta theme. v10.5 candidate.
- **Mobile Haptic Feedback** (40 LOC) — trivial but narrow (Android only). v10.5 candidate.
