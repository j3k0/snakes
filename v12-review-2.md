# Snakes v12 — Design Plan Review 2

## Verdict

**Approve with changes.** The plan is far better grounded than the v11 precedent — the great majority of its `snake.html` citations verify to the exact line, and the defects it names (swipe ignores mirror, unbounded uncleared toast queue, invisible cascade haircut, missing death cause, stale help percentages) are all real and all reproduced here. But the flagship's specification contains one internal contradiction that makes its own regression assertion *unpassable by the design it specifies* (B1), and its score effect's stated guard does not exist and its catch fails on today's build (B2). Three further verification steps cannot measure what they claim (B7, D), and three factual claims about the file are wrong (B3, B5, and the "six of the nine" tier count). **The single most important thing to fix: decide, in writing, how the 180° inversion composes with the mirror flip in `setNextDirection`, because as specified the two cancel on the x axis and the plan's catch asserts the cancelled result.** Fix B1 and B2 and the flagship is shippable; the rest is repair.

## Blocking issues

1. **`invertControls`: the mirror flip and the 180° rotation cancel on the x axis, and the plan's catch asserts the bug's value.** The plan moves the mirror flip into `setNextDirection` (L38) and puts the resonance rotation "in the same choke point" (L38, L29). Trace with `activeMutators = ['gravity','mirror']` and the window forced open: raw ArrowLeft is `−T`; mirror negates x → `+T`; the rotation negates x again → `−T`; `dirQueue` is pushed `−T` (or rejected outright as a reverse/duplicate when the head is moving horizontally, leaving the queue empty). The plan's catch at L49 expects `+TILE_SIZE` — a value produced **only** when the keydown mirror copy is *left in place* (mirror applied twice: `−T → +T → −T → +T`). So the assertion is satisfied by the exact bug the bullet names, and fails for the specified implementation. Consequence for the player under `gravity~mirror`: while the window is open, left/right behave un-mirrored — i.e. `MUTATOR_DEFS.mirror.desc` ("Left and right controls are reversed") becomes false, which is precisely the defect class this feature exists to remove. *Fix:* state the composition rule explicitly and implement inversion as a **replacement** for the mirror flip while the window is open (ArrowLeft ⇒ `+T`, matching L49 and keeping the left/right swap the player drafted), or keep both flips and change L49 to expect `−T` with a *vertical* press as the unambiguous probe (Up ⇒ Down). Separately, the safety argument must also cite the consume-time reverse check at L6508-6509: under `gravity` the head's live `dx`/`dy` is overridden each tick while `lastConsumedDir` stays at the last *queued* direction, so the push-time check at L4056 alone is not sufficient evidence that inversion cannot kill.

2. **`doubleGold`'s guard does not exist and its catch fails on today's build.** L31 claims the ×2 sits "inside the existing `Math.min(totalMult, 4)` global cap (L1860) so it cannot compound past ×4 with hoard/pigment/golden_food". L1860 caps only `multi * effectiveHoardMult * hazardChainMult`; `golden_food`'s ×3 is applied to `amount` **before** that product (L1847-1849), so it is outside the cap — as would be an `amount`-level ×2. The catch at L52 ("assert `addScore(100)` under Chaos Gold + an active hoard yields ≤400") is therefore false of the current code with no resonance at all: `100 → ×3 (golden) = 300 → effectiveHoardMult = min(2,1.5) = 1.5 → +450`. With `doubleGold` at `amount` level the same call yields 900. *Fix:* replace the catch with exact deltas under a controlled multiplier state (see the Feature A deep dive), and state whether the ×2 multiplies `amount` or enters `totalMult` — the two diverge under Pigment Rush (180 vs 120 on the same call), so the choice is observable and must be pinned.

3. **Feature C's save/death enumeration is wrong in both directions.** L99 names four "…saved you!" sites; two of them (L6591, L6610) are different strings, and three further non-death writes (L6632 "Melted a hazard!", L6648 "Vampire Brew saved you from a hazard!", L6697 "Phoenix Brew resurrected!") are missing. Counted properly there are **17** assignment sites, **7** of which are not deaths. *Fix:* split into `lastSaveEvent`/`lastDeathCause` and enumerate all seven save writes; add a running-game case that dies *immediately after* each save (melt, vampire-hazard, phoenix-wall, phoenix-rival, serpentine, streak-charge, vampire) and asserts the card names the death, not the rescue.

4. **C2's recap arithmetic is unimplementable as specified: the raw score is destroyed before `buildRecapData` runs.** `gameOver()` overwrites `score = Math.floor(score * cascMult)` at L3322 and only calls `buildRecapData()` at L3345. The planned "`Cascade ×0.5 (−250)`" line needs the pre-cascade number, and it cannot be recovered (`score/0.5` re-inflates the `Math.floor` loss and is wrong for odd scores). *Fix:* capture `sessionRawScore = score` before the multiply (or move the cascade computation into `finalizeSession`/`buildRecapData`) and have the card render `raw → final`, matching what L87 promises.

5. **Feature D's binding list and tier count are wrong.** L108 asserts the help table omits `M`, `Tab` and `F` — all three are printed today (L4169, L4172, L4173) — while omitting the twelve keys that genuinely are undocumented (`S`, `X`, `Z`, `Y`, `D`, `R`, `V`, `G`, `B`, `J`, `Q`, `K`). L108/L112 also say "nine food types"; there are **eight** `FOOD_TIERS` keys (the plan contradicts itself at L114). *Fix:* correct both lists before writing `HELP_BINDINGS`; note that the 12 missing keys include four that are context-scoped (`c` = cascade in the draft vs cosmetics in idle, L7833/L8155; `x` = combo bank in play vs prestige at idle L7954/L7965; `r` = draft re-roll vs snapshot resume L7891/L8068; `s` = draft navigation vs settings L7816/L7905), so a flat array cannot describe them without a scope column.

6. **`chaosCycle` is filed as a pass-1 "pure state/score edit" but it owns a pass-2 input site.** L42 puts `chaosCycle` in pass 1 and `invertControls`, `quantumToggle` and "the mirror-flip relocation" in pass 2 — yet `chaosCycle` gates the mirror mutator, whose only flip site is the keydown handler (L8331-8333) that pass 2 relocates. Three of the eight effects mutate that one site (`chaosCycle` gates it, `quantumToggle` sets a flag consulted by it, `invertControls` composes with it), so the pass boundary is not a verification boundary for `chaosCycle`: its pass-1 verification is invalidated the moment pass 2 lands and must be repeated. *Fix:* pull the mirror relocation (a standalone ~6-line bug fix that removes a shipped bug) out of Feature A into its own early implementation step, or move `chaosCycle` into pass 2.

7. **Feature B delegates its canvas-state catch to a Feature and a test that do not exist.** L73's catch is "the render-consistency test (Feature G)" — this plan defines only Features A–F, and no render-consistency test exists in the suite: the only canvas-related assertion is `test-feature-d.mjs` L105-113, "drawGame renders without error during playing state", which calls `drawGame(1)` once and checks it did not throw. That is precisely the shape of assertion that was green while v11 shipped transform corruption, and Feature B adds a third save/restore surface inside `drawHazards` (after L4806/L4824 and L4833/L4840) in the same function that v11 already corrupted. *Fix:* delete the dangling reference and either add a real check (assert the play-area `save`/`restore` depth is balanced at the exit of `drawGame`, or hash a deterministic seeded frame before/after) or state plainly that this failure mode is covered only by the human screenshot.

8. **The determinism strategy contradicts `quantumToggle`'s implementation.** L197 says "Any `Math.random()` introduced into a seeded path is a defect", and L40 sells the L6464 → `seededRandom()` move as fixing a determinism leak. But `quantumToggle` (L34) is specified as "call `shuffleFoodWeights()`", and `shuffleFoodWeights` draws its weights with `Math.random()` (L6031); it is already called from the tick loop at L6457. So for `mirror~chaos_seed` (and `golden_food~chaos_seed`, where L31 halves the reshuffle cadence) the seeded path stays non-deterministic by design, and any automated determinism gate keyed on "no `Math.random()` in a seeded run" will fail on runs that reach these two resonances. *Fix:* either seed `shuffleFoodWeights`' draw (a one-line change to `seededRandom()`) or scope the determinism gate to resonance pairs without `chaos_seed`, and correct L40's claim that removing L6464's draw fixes the leak — it fixes it for `gravity~mirror` only.

## Non-blocking issues

1. **`clearToasts()` as specified leaves the in-flight `setTimeout` alive.** `processToastQueue` (L7472-7488) keeps no timer handle; clearing the array and `toastActive` does not cancel the pending hide. A new-run toast queued before that timer fires gets hidden early by the stale callback, which then calls `processToastQueue()` again. Fix: generation counter (`toastGeneration`) checked inside the timeout callback, bumped by `clearToasts()`.
2. **L132's "roughly 15 seconds"** should read ~22 s (see #38 above).
3. **The cascade badge has no free slot.** Both bottom-left rows are occupied (`CANVAS_HEIGHT-28` badge L5516, `CANVAS_HEIGHT-22` XP bar L5477) and the top-right carries the mutator/resonance list (L6255); L91 specifies no coordinates. Cheapest resolution: append the factor to the existing combo-bank badge string instead of adding a second badge — one `fillText` site, no collision possible.
4. **L100's badge-collision scenario is unconstructible**: "enable cascade with 5 banked charges in daily mode with a mutator" — the draft gate is `gameMode !== 'daily'` (L7924), so a daily run can never hold a mutator or a cascade factor. Use Classic.
5. **`showFoodLegend` has two owners and its output format is unspecified.** Feature E (step 1) routes it through the queue, Feature D (step 2) rewrites its content from "one builder" — but D's builder emits weights+percentages while the legend today emits points (L7460). Specify the shared string, or the implementer will pick one and silently drop the other's information (and still omit `bomb`).
6. **`HELP_BINDINGS` is hand-maintained**, so L110's claim that the surfaces "cannot disagree" holds only for the food table; nothing prevents a new binding from missing the array. Given the plan's own rule against source-text assertions, no cheap enforcement exists — worth stating rather than implying derivation.
7. **Feature F says "three more income sources" (L156) but counts four insertions** (the Titan/Apex evolution call is a fourth). Align the wording; the LOC figure is already the four.
8. **Omission worth citing in Feature A:** L7279-7282 already contains the tick-tail guard `if (gameState !== 'playing') return;` with a comment naming the Pressure Cooker resonance by name. That is pre-existing scaffolding for `rivalShrink`'s death return, and it means the `return`-does-not-propagate hazard the plan correctly flags for `rapidDoom` is already handled for the rival path. The plan should cite it (and note that the loop at L7280-7282 does not re-check `gameState` between rivals).
9. **Test-suite implications the plan does not name.** `test-v11.mjs` L30-32, L112-113 and L265-266 assert *source text* inside `drawSnake` and `addScore` (`hazardChainMult`, `Math.min`, `pigmentRushActive`); `test-quests.mjs` L217 does the same for `updateGame`. Feature A edits `addScore` directly, so the plan's "delete rather than re-pin" policy would drop the only existing coverage of v11 A and v11 C's multipliers. Recommend re-pointing them at observable deltas (the suites already contain the right pattern: `test-v9.mjs` L224 pins `addScore(10) === 45` for golden_food+hoard, and `test-v11.mjs` L134-141 pins exact no-chain/chain scores). Also note `test-v11.mjs` L178/L233 set `dirQueue = []` and `lastConsumedDir` directly, so the relocated mirror flip must be inert when no mutators are active.
10. **`seedOffset` is a dead field.** It is written at L3645 and serialized at L2691/L2858 but never read by `tickHazards`, while AGENTS.md documents it as providing a deterministic per-hazard phase offset. Feature B is the right moment to either consume it (it is exactly the phase data a telegraph wants) or correct the doc.

## Scope assessment

Six features / ~540 LOC is not the risk driver, and neither was 7 / ~705 in v11 — the driver is **unverifiable surface**, and on that measure Feature A is roughly the whole risk. A is eight mechanics across the input path, the score path, the hunger path, the shrink path (twice), the rival path and the phase path, and four of the eight have no numeric observable in the plan's own verification (see below). The other five features are individually cheap and individually falsifiable; I would not cut any of them on size grounds.

Concretely I would:

- **Cut the flagship's scope to the half that can be proven, and gate the other half.** Ship `intangiblePhase`, `doubleGold`, `rivalShrink`, `feastFamine` with numeric assertions (hunger-pop counts, exact score/XP deltas, shrink geometry). Make `invertControls`, `quantumToggle`, `chaosCycle`, `rapidDoom` a second pass that lands *only if* its own verification passes; if it slips, v12 still converts four advertised resonances into facts, which is the issue's gap #1. If all eight must ship, buy the verification time by deferring Feature D (pure text, cannot be more wrong than it is now) — not by reducing the per-effect evidence.
- **Add nothing.** In particular, keeping the Canvas State Leak Guard out of this cycle is the right call (the plan's reasoning at L209 is sound), but do not let that decision be used to skip the one-line save/restore depth check that Feature B's own failure mode needs (B7).
- **Promote the mirror relocation out of A/step 6 into step 2.** It is a standalone bug fix, it removes a shipped defect on all three input paths, and every later input-touching effect depends on it being correct. Landing it last means steps 3-6 are built on, and verified against, the buggy foundation.
- **Prerequisite check:** nothing in the six features creates a new invariant; the ordering hazards are all "two features edit the same function" (E and D both own `showFoodLegend`; A's two passes and chaosCycle all own the mirror site; C's badge and E's clear both touch `resetGame`). One owner per function, named in the implementation plan, is the missing prerequisite.

**Most likely to ship broken-but-green: `doubleGold`.** Its observable is a number that is already multiplied by three other systems (golden_food ×3 L1847, hoard ×2 / pigment ×3 L1852-1857, hazard chain ×2.5 L1859) and clipped by a cap that does not cover it; its XP half has no HUD at all and feeds a *persistent* multiplier; and the plan's own catch for it is falsified by today's build, so it cannot be used at all. A `doubleGold` that is silently absorbed by the cap, or that is applied to XP only, or that multiplies `totalMult` instead of `amount`, passes any "score went up" assertion. `invertControls` is the runner-up for the same reason in reverse: its stated catch is only satisfiable by the bug, so an implementer who "fixes" the code to satisfy it ships the double-mirror defect.

**The verification that catches it:** with `gameMode='classic'`, no prestige, no hoard, no pigment rush and no hazard adjacency, read `score` before and after one forced eat of a known tier and assert the *exact* delta: `common` under `doubleGold` must be `+60` (10 ×3 golden ×2), and under the same conditions `sessionXP` must rise by `2 × FOOD_TIERS.common.xp` (50). Then repeat once with `pigmentRushActive` and assert the same number the implementation is specified to produce (180 if the ×2 is on `amount`, 120 if it is inside `totalMult`), plus `totalMult ≤ 4`. That is three numbers that pin the placement of the multiplier unambiguously — and it is the shape the suite already uses (`test-v9.mjs` L224, `test-v11.mjs` L134-141). Note this only works *after* B2's catch is replaced.
